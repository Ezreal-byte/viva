A to-do app, 480x600: a title, a text field with an Add button beside it that appends the field's text as a task, then a scrolling list of tasks, each row with a checkbox, the task text taking the free room (muted color when done) and a small ghost Delete button at the right end; under the list a footer line '3 left' counting unfinished tasks and a 'Clear done' ghost button. Start with three sample tasks, none of them done yet.
