A gallery, 800x520. On the left a grid of 12 square-ish coloured tiles that fills the free width and wraps to as many columns as fit (each at least 110 px wide and 90 px tall); tile n is filled with a colour from the list Red, Orange, Green, Blue, Purple, Pink, repeated (tile 1 Red, 2 Orange, 3 Green, 4 Blue, 5 Purple, 6 Pink, 7 Red, ...), shows its number in white and has the accessible name 'Tile n'. On the right a fixed 240 px panel: with nothing selected it says 'Select a tile'; after a click on a tile it shows 'Selected: Tile 5', a large swatch of the tile's colour and 'Colour: Purple', and a 'Clear selection' outline button that empties the selection. The selected tile has a thick border. Start with nothing selected.
