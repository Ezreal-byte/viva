A theme colour picker, 640x420: a title 'Accent colour', a row of six rounded square swatches, one per colour, in this order: Red, Orange, Green, Blue, Purple, Pink. Each swatch is clickable, filled with its colour, and its accessible name is the colour's name; the selected one has a thick border. Blue is selected at start. Below it a preview card with a heading 'Preview', a button 'Sample button' filled with the chosen colour, a line 'Selected: Blue' (the chosen colour's name), and a Switch named 'Dark preview' with a muted text beside it: 'Dark preview: off' or 'Dark preview: on'; when on the card's background is dark and its text light. Clicking a swatch changes the button, the border and the text at once.
