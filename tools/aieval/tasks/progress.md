A page, 560x300: a title 'Upload', a 'Start' button, and below it a progress bar and a percentage text. After Start is clicked the bar fills smoothly from 0 to 100 percent over about 3 seconds, the percentage text counting along, and a small spinner (a rotating arc or dots) turns beside the bar while it runs; both stop when it is done, and the page then says 'Done'. The window must not repaint when nothing is running.
