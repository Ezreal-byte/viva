An email client, 860x520, three columns. Left (180 px): the folders as clickable rows 'Inbox (3)', 'Sent (0)' and 'Archive (0)', each showing its number of unread messages (the selected folder is highlighted; Inbox at start). Middle (280 px): the messages of the selected folder as a scrolling list of rows, each with the subject (bold while unread) and under it the sender's name in muted small type (just the name). Right: the reading pane, with 'Select a message' until one is clicked; then the subject large, 'From: <name>', the body text and a 'Move to archive' button. Inbox holds, in this order: 'Welcome to Viva' from Ana, 'Lunch on Friday?' from Ben, 'Invoice 4821' from Billing, all unread; Archive holds 'Old newsletter' from News and 'Trip photos' from Mia, both read; Sent is empty. Opening a message marks it read (the folder count drops) and moving it to the archive removes it from the list, clears the reading pane, and adds it to Archive, where it counts as read.
