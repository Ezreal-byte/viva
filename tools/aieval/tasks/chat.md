A chat window, 480x600, title 'Support chat'. The conversation fills the space between the title and the bottom and scrolls: the other person's messages are bubbles on the left (muted background), mine are bubbles on the right (primary color, white text), each bubble at most 70% of the width and wrapping long text. Start with 'Hi! How can I help you today?' (them), 'My export keeps failing' (me) and 'Which format are you exporting?' (them). At the bottom, always visible, a row with a text field (placeholder 'Type a message') taking the free width and a Send button. Send, or Enter in the field, appends what was typed as a bubble on the right, empties the field, and then the other side answers at once with 'Thanks, we will look into it.' on the left. An empty or blank message is ignored.
